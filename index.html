Critical Decision Points

1. Court Initial Response (48 Hours)

  • Option A: Additional documents required → Loop back to submission
  • Option B: Approval with payment link → Proceed to payment

2. Judgment Type

  • Final Judgment: Case ends immediately
  • Primary Judgment: Expert appointment process begins

3. Expert Fee Payment (1 Week Deadline)

  • Paid: 4-week timeline to next hearing + expert process
  • Not Paid: Final hearing on pre-set date → Final judgment → Case closed

4. Court Date Timing

  • Original date not reached: Use original scheduled date
  • Original date passed: Court sets new date

Process Optimization Recommendations

Action Required by Client:

Immediate Document Upload/Submission:

  1. ID Copy - Submit immediately upon engagement
  2. All Written Communications with the other party:
    • Emails (complete thread history)
    • SMS messages (screenshots)
    • Physical letters (scanned copies)
    • Any other correspondence
  3. Recent AECB Report (if already obtained)
    • If not available, we will advise if necessary for your specific case

Critical Client Responsibilities:

  1. Review Detailed Appeal Memo - Provide comments within 24 hours of receiving
  2. Monitor Payment Deadlines - Respond immediately to court payment requests (48-hour window)
  3. UAE Pass Access - Ensure your UAE Pass is active and accessible
  4. Document Organization - Maintain copies of all submissions and court communications

Timeline Awareness:

  • Expert fees are typically paid by the other party (bank)
  • Keep all original documents organized and accessible
  • Maintain timeline awareness for all court-imposed deadlines

Critical Milestones to Track:

  • UAE Pass login successful
  • User account created/accessible
  • Case linked to user account (if required)
  • Registration request submitted
  • Court response received (48 hours)
  • Payment completed
  • Detailed appeal memo reviewed and approved
  • First hearing attended
  • Judgment type determined
  • Expert fees status confirmed (paid by other party)
  • Expert sessions completed (if applicable)
  • Final comments submitted
  • Final verdict received
